### Lesson Learned

*Lesson from usability testing:* The 3-price limit above the fold came directly from UT: it showed people skim rather than read closely while comparing wholesale prices, so packing in more numbers just added noise. The same round of testing also surfaced a habit that shaped the cart design — people were adding items to cart just to simulate the final price, treating the cart as a pricing calculator, not necessarily a purchase intent. That's why a "next cheaper price" nudge was added directly in cart, meeting that habit instead of fighting it.
